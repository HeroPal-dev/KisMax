# KisMax (KM)

An addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever** that works on top of
[Naowh Forever](https://github.com/naowh-gaming/NaowhForever)'s Professions window.
It does not change or copy Naowh's files: it uses Naowh's public tables and hooks its functions.

Requires **NaowhForever** and **NaowhForever_Professions**. Works better with **TradeSkillMaster** (prices) and
**NaowhForever_QoL** (auction scan).

## What it does

- **Profit in the recipe list**: per craft, ROI, the whole buy plan, or per hour of crafting; sale channel:
  auction house, merchant / disenchant, or the best of them.
- **Buy plan** under the profession window: how many crafts pay with today's lots (cheapest first), your money
  and bag space; what to buy and up to which price.
- **Shopping list** with price limits, handed to Naowh's buyer; merchant purchases with one button.
- **Session plan**: several profitable recipes sharing your money, bags and lots; then Naowh's crafting queue.
- **Levelling plan**: the cheapest way to a target skill for any profession, with recipe and rank costs,
  where to learn them (waypoints), and what crafted items bring back.
- **Profession overview**: every recipe of every profession by profit per hour.
- **Disenchant values** on item tooltips; disenchanting as a way to sell.
- **Ledger and history by day** of what crafting really cost and earned.
- **KisMax window** (minimap button, `/km`) with the tools and all settings.

## Commands

`/km` opens the KisMax window, `/km help` lists the commands (`/kismax` and the old `/npp` work too).
